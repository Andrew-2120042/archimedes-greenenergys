About Us

Hyderabad-based pioneers of India's first rooftop wind turbine for sustainable urban energy.

India's first rooftop wind turbine — built on 20 years of Dutch R&D.

Archimedes Green Energys (P) Ltd. was established in 2016 by CEO Suryaprakash Gajjala — a technocrat entrepreneur inspired by the Make in India initiative and recipient of the MSME National Award from the Ministry of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises, Govt. of India.

The technology is an outcome of 20 years of R&D by Dutch scientist Marinus Mieremet, who perfected the Archimedes screw principle into a new and more efficient form of wind power generation.

The AWM will be manufactured commercially in partnership with Korean ESCO RTS and Netherlands technology — a state-of-the-art plant at Medchal with capacity of one lakh units per annum, designed as a global export unit.

The Technology

The Archimedes screw principle, reborn as a wind turbine.

The design takes after the Archimedes screw pump — a helix-patterned spiral — with blades that fan out from front to back in the shape of a cone. This allows the turbine to swivel and collect gusts entering at angles as wide as 60° from its axis.

Unlike traditional HAWTs which rely solely on lift force, the AWM uses both lift and drag forces together — extracting significantly more kinetic energy from the wind, even at very low urban wind speeds.

Urban Deployment

Built for the city. Works everywhere wind blows.

Wind direction in urban environments changes constantly — but the Archimedes wind turbine follows the wind automatically and operates efficiently at low speeds. The 2.8-meter AWM under development will approach 4,000W at maximum efficiency.

A hybrid energy control system is also under development to optimise energy storage combining wind and solar power — compensating for solar's daytime-only generation with stored wind energy for night-time use.
